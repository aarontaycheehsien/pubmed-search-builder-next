- **Condition:** neonatal sepsis, bloodstream infection, and bacteremia.
- **Assay/performance:** molecular diagnostics, nucleic-acid amplification and hybridization/probe techniques, PCR families, and diagnostic performance.
- **Date restriction:** publication date through 2014-08-09, per the run's historical cutoff.

## NCBI CLI work performed

The bundled CLI was used for MeSH lookup, descriptor and tree inspection, and PubMed searches. The initial GET-based CLI call for the full query returned HTTP 414 because the URL exceeded the endpoint limit. The same CLI was copied to `work/ncbi_cli_post.py` with only the request method changed to POST; the full query and candidate checks then ran successfully. Working files and raw JSON outputs are under `work/cli/`.

#### Other descriptors considered

- **Neonatal Sepsis** was inspected and excluded as a MeSH heading because its descriptor record says it was introduced in 2017, after the cutoff. Its concept remains covered by the newborn and sepsis blocks plus free text.
- **Molecular Probes** was excluded as a generic research-probe heading; the more specific DNA Probes, RNA Probes, and Nucleic Acid Hybridization headings were included.
- **Molecular Diagnostic Techniques** has HPV DNA Tests and Comparative Genomic Hybridization as narrower descriptors; explosion is retained for sensitivity.
- **Nucleic Acid Amplification Techniques** has relevant narrower methods (PCR, real-time PCR, multiplex PCR, reverse-transcriptase PCR, NASBA/self-sustained sequence replication, and ligase chain reaction) plus less directly relevant methods. The clinically relevant PCR/amplification children are included explicitly; the parent remains exploded for sensitivity.
- **Nucleic Acid Hybridization** has children including branched-DNA signal amplification and fluorescence/in-situ hybridization, alongside broader laboratory methods. It remains exploded to retain molecular detection approaches; precision is helped by the population and condition blocks.
- **Sensitivity and Specificity** is included as `[Mesh:noexp]` to avoid expansion into wider measurement topics. Its entry terms are included in the assay/performance OR block.

## Rationale

- Three essential concept blocks keep the search focused on newborns with sepsis/bloodstream infection evaluated using molecular assays.
- Reference standards and diagnostic-study design were not made separate `AND` blocks. Those criteria are applied during screening; requiring them in the query could miss records with sparse abstracts or indexing.
- Diagnostic performance is an OR arm (`Sensitivity and Specificity`) to add recall without requiring every record to carry that heading.
- No language, publication-type, species, or methodological filters were applied. The publication-date limit is the only date restriction and is included to honor the run's historical cutoff.
- `[Mesh]` headings are explicit. MeSH headings in this strategy were available by the cutoff; the newer Neonatal Sepsis descriptor was excluded. MeSH trees and the online index may now contain later additions, so the search should be checked against a 2014 archived MeSH/PubMed environment if exact historical reproducibility is needed.
